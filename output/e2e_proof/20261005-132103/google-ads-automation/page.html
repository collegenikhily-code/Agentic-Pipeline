Frequently Asked Questions

What Google Ads automation features are available?

Google Ads offers automated bidding strategies including Target CPA, Target ROAS, Maximize Conversions, and Enhanced CPC. These use machine learning to set bids for each auction based on conversion probability and contextual signals.

How does automated bidding protect against wasted spend?

Automated systems apply bid caps, audience exclusions, and budget constraints defined in your rules. You retain full control over spending limits and can pause automation at any campaign level.

What data sources integrate with Google Ads automation?

Google Analytics 4 provides conversion tracking, while CRM platforms like Salesforce push lead quality signals. Third-party bid management tools aggregate cross-account data for centralized rule execution.

Is Google Ads automation suitable for small accounts?

Automated bidding works for accounts with sufficient conversion volume, typically 15+ conversions per month per campaign. Small accounts benefit from rule-based automation even with lower volume.

How do I audit automated changes?

Google Ads logs all automated bid changes in the audit history. Export these logs weekly to review which rules fired and verify alignment with your performance targets.
